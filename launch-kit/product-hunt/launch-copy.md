## Maker comment

Hey Product Hunt — I built LetDue after noticing that small landlords are expected to remember a growing list of certificate and renewal dates, while most property software is designed to do far more than they need.

LetDue has one job: keep the evidence organised and warn you before something important expires.

You can run a property audit without creating an account, upload a certificate instead of typing its dates, and see immediately what is current, due soon, overdue or missing. If you want ongoing monitoring, LetDue sends reminders 90, 30, 14 and 7 days before the deadline, plus on the due date.

It is deliberately focused on self-managing landlords with one to three properties in England. It does not collect rent, manage tenants or try to replace a letting agent.

The founding plan is £29 a year, with a free 14-day pilot and no card required.

I would especially value feedback on:

1. Which recurring property document is easiest to lose track of?
2. Is the free audit clear enough for a first-time landlord?
3. What would make you trust a compliance reminder enough to rely on it?

Thanks for taking a look.
