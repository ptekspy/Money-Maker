# LetDue partner-forward one-pager

Use this when a certificate supplier, agent or landlord-service partner is willing to forward a useful resource to one small landlord. Do not bulk send.

## Forwardable note

Subject: A simple reminder tool for certificate renewal dates

Hi {{landlord_name}},

I thought this might be useful after your certificate/report.

LetDue is a small reminder tool for self-managing landlords in England. You enter or upload certificate dates such as gas safety, EICR, EPC, insurance or licence renewals, and it turns them into a reminder calendar.

Free first step:
`https://letdue.com/?source={{partner_source}}`

If the audit is useful, the founding plan is £29/year for up to three properties:
`https://letdue.com/founding-plan?source={{partner_source}}`

It is not a letting platform and does not replace professional advice. It is just a way to keep documents, dates and reminders in one place.
