## Five useful X posts

1. A certificate spreadsheet usually fails at the handover: the renewed PDF arrives, but the old date stays in the sheet. A reliable system has to keep the document, date and next reminder together.

2. Portfolio admin question: if the person who maintains your certificate spreadsheet disappeared for a month, would someone else know what expires next?

3. Full property-management software can be excellent. But if the actual problem is “where are the certificates and what expires next?”, buying the whole platform may be unnecessary.

4. A sensible certificate migration does not start with every historic document. Start with the next renewals, verify the dates against the originals, then work backwards property by property.

5. LetDue now supports 10, 50 and 100-property portfolios. If your certificate dates currently live across spreadsheets, calendars and email, I will review the setup personally: https://letdue.com/portfolio-health-check?source=x-portfolio-review
